# Features List – Fair Accounting App (Draft v0.1)

Working title: Book Fair Accounting App · Draft prepared 30 Oct 2026

Built only from your two uploaded chat files, which contain your messages only. Where a feature depends on a missing question or an undecided point, it is marked and not assumed.

## 1. Confirmed features

### Fair and school setup

- Every fair is linked to a **host school** (you called this a basic, good functionality).
- A school can have **multiple branches**.
- Balances are tracked **fair-wise**, a hybrid of customer and location in QBO terms.

### Customer details

- Maintain **customer details**.

### Free books

- Free books are handled on a **percentage basis**, as decided with the school.
- The percentage is **entered by the user**; the app does not set it.

### Accounting

- Accounting principles and custom accounting logic are **maintained by the user**.
- Bills are expected in the **500–1000 range** (what this counts is an open item).

### Flexibility and notes

- Users can add **extra fields** with **no limit**.
- A **notes section** lets users record specific notes.
- Records can be **edited or deleted**.

### Data and platform

- **Export** and **backup**, both required.
- **English only**.
